What is Frunchy?

Frunchy was born out of "healthy envy" when I first saw Steam's _recap_. I watched it and thought, "How nice," and wondered why _YouTube_ didn't have something similar. Then I realized that I have _YouTube_'s _history_ turned off—and it’s still off, haha. After that, I wondered why _Crunchyroll_ didn't have one. This was around 2020, and I thought, "It would be really cool if Crunchyroll had one." And, like any good programmer (haha), if it doesn't exist, you make it, because we're the kind of people who solve problems (ha, ha, ha).

So, in 2020 I started working on the Chrome extension. I met with a friend to help me with the design, since I wanted it to be informative and impactful. After I had a _POC_ showing that I could read Crunchyroll's _history_ and ingest it into Falco (yes, I used [Falco](https://www.falcoframework.com) at the time), I was satisfied and told myself, "Well, I'll pick this up again later..."

Then came procrastination, more work, more _POCs_, and Frunchy was left on the shelf 😕

In 2025, Crunchyroll created a nice _recap_, but I think it only appeared on my phone once and never again... At that point I thought, "Oh, man, I should have made it."

In 2026, I embraced AI. I started using it on work projects, and everything just kept flowing. I remembered Frunchy and thought, "That might be nice," and now we have Frunchy 😊

The decision to build Frunchy (I think you can still call it programming when you do it with an agent) helped me finally tackle something I'd been putting off for a long time: getting my own server. I already had the `lacalabaza.xyz` domain, but I still needed the server. I had Hetzner on my list, but by the time I checked, they had discontinued the _Cost Optimized_ instances. So began my search for _VMs_, and in the end I settled on _[Netcup](https://netcup.com)_.

Then came more questions and answers, which led to an avalanche of new projects I'm really enjoying. I'll talk about them in more depth in the future. The projects are [Umbral](https://github.com/cosmic-chimps/umbral), [Caravana](https://github.com/cosmic-chimps/caravana), and [Megáfono](https://github.com/cosmic-chimps/megafono).

[Frunchy](https://frunchy.lacalabaza.xyz) is currently invite-only. I'm testing it with some friends. The code is open source and available in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/jjchiw/frunchy), where you'll find instructions on how to install it.

Here's the [Chrome extension repository](https://github.com/jjchiw/frunchy-chrome-extension).

Cheers.
