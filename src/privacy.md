This policy covers lacalabaza.xyz and Umbral, the single sign-on service I run at lacalabaza.xyz. Umbral lets me, and the people I invite, sign in to my own self-hosted apps with a Google account.

## What this website collects

Nothing. The pages are static, there is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking scripts. Like any web server, mine keeps ordinary access logs for a short time to keep the site running and secure.

## What Umbral reads from Google

When you sign in with Google, Umbral reads three things from your Google account:

- your email address
- your name
- your profile picture

It does not read your mail, contacts, files, calendar or anything else in your Google account.

## How it is used

Only to create and recognize your account and to sign you in to the apps I host. Nothing else is done with it.

## Where it is stored

On my own server. It is not shared with anyone and it is not sold. No advertising companies, analytics services or other third parties receive it. Google itself handles the sign-in step under its own privacy policy.

## Deleting your data

To have your account and its data deleted, email {{ site.contactEmail }} and I will remove it. You can also revoke Umbral's access at any time from your Google account's security settings.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its date.
