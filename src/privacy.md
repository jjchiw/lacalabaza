This policy covers lacalabaza.xyz and all the services under it.

## What this website collects

Nothing. The pages are static, there is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking scripts. Like any web server, mine keeps ordinary access logs for a short time to keep the site running and secure.

## What Umbral reads

When you sign in, Umbral reads three things from your Google account:

- your email address
- your name
- your profile picture

It does not read your mail, contacts, files, calendar or anything else in your Google account.

## How it is used

Only to create and recognize your account and to sign you in to the apps I host. Nothing else is done with it.

## Where it is stored

On my own server. It is not shared with anyone and it is not sold. No advertising companies, analytics services or other third parties receive it.

## Deleting your data

To have your account and its data deleted, email {{ site.contactEmail }} and I will remove it. You can also revoke la calabaza access at any time from your SSO account's security settings.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its date.
